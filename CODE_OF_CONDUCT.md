# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Participation must remain free from harassment regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity
and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while evaluating technical claims on
  evidence.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections professionally.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and respect privacy.
- Credit the work of others and disclose conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, or
  repeated unwanted contact.
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or publishing another
  person's private information without permission.
- Abuse of project infrastructure, vulnerability reports, or support channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to `1061517027@qq.com` with relevant links
or screenshots. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible. This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.
