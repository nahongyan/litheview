# Security policy

## Supported releases

Security fixes are provided for the latest stable LitheView SDK release.
Older releases may receive a fix when required by a separate commercial
support agreement. Users should update to the latest stable release before
reporting an issue that may already be resolved.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Send a report to
`1061517027@qq.com` with the subject `LitheView security report` and include:

- affected SDK version and platform;
- impact and the conditions required to reproduce it;
- a minimal reproduction or proof of concept when safe to share;
- whether the issue is already public;
- your preferred contact details and disclosure timeline.

Avoid accessing data that is not yours, disrupting services, or testing
against third-party systems without permission. Do not include secrets or
personal data that are unnecessary for the report.

We will aim to acknowledge a complete report within five business days,
provide an initial assessment within ten business days, and coordinate a fix
and disclosure timeline based on severity. These targets are not a service
level agreement.

Good-faith research that follows this policy will not be treated as a license
violation solely because limited reverse engineering was necessary to identify
and report the vulnerability, where applicable law permits that research.
